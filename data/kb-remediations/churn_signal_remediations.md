# Churn Signal Remediations

## Symptom: High Volume of Churn Signals (>75 in 2 minutes)

### Root Causes & Fixes

**Content Drought**
If churn signals correlate with players who have completed all available content (max level, all campaigns cleared), the game needs fresh content. Deploy a limited-time event or challenge within 24 hours. Activate the emergency content pipeline (recycled events with new rewards).

**Difficulty Spike**
If churn signals are concentrated in a specific level range or game mode, a difficulty spike is pushing players out. Temporarily reduce difficulty for the affected content. Add hint systems or optional assists. Offer a one-time resource boost to stuck players.

**Social Feature Degradation**
If churn signals correlate with players in inactive guilds or with low social interaction scores, the social fabric is weakening. Send targeted guild recruitment notifications. Activate guild merge suggestions for undersized guilds. Launch a social event that rewards cooperative play.

**Economy Imbalance**
If churn signals correlate with free-to-play players who haven't purchased, the economy may be too punishing for non-spenders. Increase daily free currency rewards by 20-30%. Add a "welcome back" bonus for returning players. Review and adjust energy regeneration rates.

**Competitive Frustration**
If churn signals come from players with declining arena ranks or high loss streaks, matchmaking may be unfair. Review matchmaking algorithm for power-level mismatches. Implement loss streak protection (reduced rank loss after 3+ consecutive losses). Add a casual/unranked mode option.

## Preventive Measures

Build a churn prediction model that identifies at-risk players 7 days before they churn. Implement automated re-engagement campaigns triggered by declining login frequency. Maintain a library of "win-back" offers segmented by player type (whale, dolphin, minnow, free-to-play).
